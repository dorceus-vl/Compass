Compass · pick a directionThree ways Compass could feel

Same colours, same cards, three kinds of motion.

Each direction keeps the ivory accent, the dark ground and the card shapes you liked, and adds a different personality on top: things you can tilt, things that move as you scroll, or a cockpit you operate. Everything below is live: move your mouse, scroll, tap. The tasks and projects are examples.

Direction A

Depth: cards you can almost hold

Space and light. The home screen has layered depth that follows your pointer, your three horizons sit on a 3D carousel you spin, and every card tilts toward your cursor with a soft ivory glare.

  • Best for: a premium, tactile feel on the Mac and iPad.
  • On the phone: the carousel swipes; cards tilt with a press.

Direction B

Flow: your day as a story you scroll

Motion tied to your thumb. A compass needle turns as you move through the day, the timeline draws itself and the task in the centre comes into focus, milestones light up along a path, and projects stack like a deck as you pass them.

  • Best for: the phone, where scrolling is the main gesture.
  • Feels calm: nothing moves unless you scroll.

Direction C

Instrument: a cockpit for your work

Frosted glass over a slow ivory aurora. Your horizons live on a 3D compass dial whose needle swings to the one you pick, project cards flip over to show their risk map, and finishing a task gives a small burst.

  • Best for: a strong identity that matches the Compass name.
  • The most "app-like" of the three; the most work to build.
