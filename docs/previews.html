The three accents

Ivory #EDE3C9, the snowy owl. Recommended: a task board is full of status colour (red blocked, amber due, green done, seven app colours), so a calm accent keeps the signals readable. 16:1 contrast on the ground.

Needle red #FF4D6D, the compass needle. Most energy, but it fights the "overdue / blocked" red on every card.

Compass gold #E9C46A, the brass of an old compass. Warm and handsome, but sits close to Hollow's amber in a tab bar of app icons.

What does PRINCE2 say about tolerances here?
PRINCE2 manages by exception: you set tolerances for time, cost, scope, quality, risk and benefits, and only escalate when a forecast breaches one PRINCE2 › Principles › Manage by exception. A 6-day outage three weeks from cutover almost certainly forecasts a time breach, so this goes to the project board as an exception report, not a status line.
