The status report (rapport d'avancement) is the template you will generate most often. Compass shows it as a preview on the project's Report tab and builds it on your device for the period you pick: this week, the last two weeks, this month or custom dates. You download it as Word, Markdown or PDF; the same report also appears in the Templates list below the preview. Almost every section is filled from the project's own data, so a weak report usually points to something on the project page that needs attention, not to the document. What a good report says, and how to set RAG thresholds, is covered in [[status-reporting]]; this guide goes through the sections in order.

## Overall status

Three fields: the RAG, its reason, and the period.

- **Comes from**: the RAG and the RAG reason on the project page, and the range you chose.
- **Good**: a reason a busy executive can act on, with the trend: "Amber, getting worse: wave 2 equipment held at customs; four cutovers at risk next week."
- **Mistakes**: a blank reason, which leaves the reader guessing; a reason that describes activity ("working with vendor").

## Executive summary

On a project that allows cloud AI, "Write the summary with Athena" drafts this section from the report's data. Otherwise, Compass prints the progress line: tasks done this period, done out of total with a percentage, and the number overdue.

- **Good**: three to five sentences: where the project stands, what changed since last time, what you need and from whom. Edit Athena's draft or the progress line in Word until it says that.
- **Mistakes**: sending the bare task count to a sponsor; a summary that ends without the ask.

## Progress

A list of the tasks marked done within the period.

- **Good**: task titles that read as results ("Site acceptance signed, 6 of 12 wave 2 sites"), because the list is printed as written. Titles that work on your board also work here when they name an output.
- **Mistakes**: titles only you understand ("follow up w/ JP"); a period so long that the list runs to a page.

## Next steps

The open tasks due in the next two weeks.

- **Good**: the few things the reader would want to know are coming, with dates. If a key step is missing, it is usually missing a due date on the board.
- **Mistakes**: twenty routine tasks drowning the two that matter. Split or merge tasks on the board rather than editing the list every week.

## Milestones

A table of the project's milestones, each with its due date and a state: Done, Late, Due this period or Upcoming.

- **Good**: a short list of summary milestones with gates marked ◆ on the project page and their outcomes recorded in Decisions. A Late milestone should already have a line in the RAG reason and an item in the RAID log.
- **Mistakes**: a milestone shown Late for weeks with nothing said about it; done milestones never ticked, so they show as Late.

## Risks and issues

Two tables from the RAID tab: open risks, highest score first, and open issues.

- **Good**: every row has an owner and a real response. For a steering committee, trim the Word version to the risks that need its attention; the working group can have the full lists.
- **Mistakes**: risks nobody has rescored for a month; issues already resolved but never closed on the tab.

## Decisions needed

The project's pending change requests, by title.

- **Good**: each title says what decision is needed, so it can be read alone: "Approve two extra night cutover windows for wave 3". Put the most important one in the executive summary too.
- **Mistakes**: change requests left pending after a verbal decision; other decisions you need (a design choice, a budget release) left out. Add those as a line in Word, or log them as change requests if they change scope, time or cost.

## Decisions made

A table of the decisions logged in the Decisions tab during the period, with their dates.

- **Good**: decisions written so they stand alone: who decided what, and any condition attached. Gate outcomes belong here.
- **Mistakes**: decisions taken in meetings but never logged, so the report suggests nothing was decided.

## In practice

- Fill in the RAG reason on every active project before generating its report.
- Pick "Last 2 weeks" for a fortnightly report so the Progress list matches what the reader last saw.
- Rename three board tasks so they read as results.
- Close any RAID item that is no longer open, then regenerate.
- Log this week's decisions in the Decisions tab before sending the report.
